Yunnan Baiyao × Water Margin

云南白药 × 水浒传

这是一组为大学生广告大赛创作的品牌案例，将中国四大名著《水浒传》与云南白药的产品语言重新组合，通过瓦楞纸手工艺 × AI 动画的方式，输出两支具有仪式感与东方美学的短片：《武松打虎》与《误入白虎堂》。 A brand campaign entry for a university-level advertising competition. The project reinterprets Water Margin — one of China's four classic novels — through the visual language of Yunnan Baiyao, using corrugated paper craft combined with AI animation to produce two ceremonial, orientally styled shorts: "Wu Song Fights the Tiger" and "Trespassing the White Tiger Hall."

项目概念Concept

《水浒传》是中国传统叙事中英雄气质最鲜明的文本之一；云南白药作为百年国民品牌，本身也带有浓厚的"守护"与"力量"隐喻。本项目将两者的东方精神拉到同一条视觉线上，用瓦楞纸手工艺塑造角色与场景骨架，再用 AI 动画完成运动与镜头语言，得到一种介于装置艺术与商业广告之间的国潮短片形式。Water Margin is one of the most iconic hero-driven texts in Chinese classical literature; Yunnan Baiyao, as a century-old national brand, carries strong metaphors of protection and strength. This project aligns both through a single visual language: corrugated paper craft shapes characters and scenes, while AI animation drives movement and cinematic framing, producing a Guochao short-film style that sits between installation art and brand advertising.

两支短片各自围绕一个经典章节：《武松打虎》强调力量与保护，与云南白药的"守护力"呼应；《误入白虎堂》强调秩序与清白，与产品线中的"光钻白 · 光净"叙事相扣。Each short focuses on a classic chapter: "Wu Song Fights the Tiger" foregrounds strength and protection, echoing Yunnan Baiyao's protective proposition; "Trespassing the White Tiger Hall" foregrounds order and clarity, tying into the Pro Shining White product narrative.

从原著章节提炼、品牌概念对齐、纸艺设计到 AI 动画与最终剪辑，六个阶段串起整个案例。Six stages structure the entire case, from source-chapter distillation and brand alignment to paper-craft design, AI animation, and final editing.

01章节提炼Chapter Distillation

从《水浒传》中筛选与云南白药气质匹配的两个高识别度段落。Selecting two highly recognizable Water Margin chapters that align with Yunnan Baiyao's brand character.

02品牌对齐Brand Alignment

把两支短片的情绪主线锚定到"守护"与"清白"两条品牌关键词。Anchoring each short's emotional line to the brand keywords of "protection" and "clarity."

为角色、道具与场景绘制瓦楞纸手工艺造型，确定统一材质与色系。Designing corrugated-paper forms for characters, props, and scenes with a unified material and palette.

04关键帧生成Keyframe Generation

在纸艺形象稳定后再进入 AI 关键帧，保证品牌视觉一致性。Only after paper-craft designs are locked do we move into AI keyframe generation, keeping brand visuals consistent.

06剪辑与整合Editing & Integration

最终通过剪辑节奏、音乐与产品收束，把两支短片打磨为成片。Editing rhythm, music, and product resolution finally polish both shorts into finished films.
