项目概述Overview

《黑山》以美国中部森林中的独居伐木工为主角，从日常劳作切入，在低调而压抑的节奏中逐步引出未知威胁。影片通过异响、异常动物反应、窥视感镜头与追逐段落持续累积悬念，并在结尾处揭示主角隐藏的机械身份，使作品从森林惊悚转向具有科幻意味的反转叙事。Set around an isolated lumberjack in a forest, *Black Mountain* begins with routine labor and gradually introduces an unknown threat through ominous sound, animal disruption, voyeuristic framing, and a pursuit sequence. The final reveal of the protagonist's mechanical identity reframes the film from woodland horror into a sci-fi twist.

项目重点在于：以镜头语言建立惊悚节奏，以角色与场景锚点保证生成一致性，以最终剪辑与音效完成整支短片的视听整合。The project focuses on suspenseful shot design, consistency through character and scene anchoring, and final audio-visual integration through editing and sound.

ChatGPT 生图策略ChatGPT Image Strategy

我主要参考预先建立的角色库、角色身份、场景风格，以及手绘分镜和脑中的镜头想象，再加入少量描述让 ChatGPT 在明确边界内进行创造。目标不是机械复制，而是在稳定角色与气质的前提下，保留图像生成的发挥空间。I guide ChatGPT image generation with a predefined character library, identity anchors, scene style, hand-drawn boards, and imagined shots, then add minimal description to allow controlled creative expansion rather than rigid reproduction.

Seedance 2.0 视频策略Seedance 2.0 Video Strategy

短于 5 秒或动作较少的镜头，我重点控制镜头运动与专业镜头术语；较长镜头则会精确到每秒的运镜、人物站位、动作推进和表演变化，让视频生成更接近导演式控制而不是单镜头碰运气。For short or low-motion shots, I focus on camera movement and precise cinematic terms. For longer shots, I describe movement, blocking, action progression, and performance second by second to direct the sequence more intentionally.
