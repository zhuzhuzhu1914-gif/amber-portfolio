锈剑与星尘 / Rusted Sword and Stardust

一个以文学幻想与哲学叙事为核心的纯手绘角色设计、场景设计与世界观设定练习。项目围绕骑士、女巫、教会与古代遗迹展开，通过锈剑、星尘与废墟意象讨论秩序、牺牲与新纪元的诞生。 A purely hand-drawn character and environment design practice centered on literary fantasy and philosophical storytelling. Built around a knight, a sorceress, the Church, and ancient ruins, the project uses rusted swords, stardust, and monuments of collapse to explore order, sacrifice, and the birth of a new era.

项目概述Overview

本项目是一个以“反传统英雄叙事”为核心的纯手绘角色与场景设计练习。故事中，骑士长莱恩奉教会命令追捕女巫艾琳，试图夺回“反星之铁”，却在古代魔塔中发现教会供奉的圣铃竟是吞噬星辰以维系平衡的怪物。最终，两人在废墟与裂隙中摧毁旧秩序，并以炼金术共同保护世界，留下锈剑与星尘融合后的新矿石作为新时代的象征。This project is a purely hand-drawn character and environment design exercise built around an anti-traditional heroic narrative. Knight Captain Laine pursues the sorceress Eileen under Church orders to reclaim the “Iron Against the Stars,” only to discover that the Church’s sacred bell is a monstrous being that consumes stars to sustain balance. In the end, they destroy the old order and protect the world through alchemy, leaving behind a new ore born from rusted steel and stardust.

设计重点Design Focus

整体风格强调文学叙事感、象征主义和废墟史诗气质。场景上突出魔塔、仪式圆盘、废墟与新生矿石；材质上强调锈蚀金属与发光星尘的对比；角色层面则围绕骑士、女巫、暴君与实验怪物建立权力与信仰的冲突结构。The visual direction emphasizes literary narrative, symbolism, and ruin-epic atmosphere. Scene design highlights the magic tower, ritual disc, ruins, and newborn ore, while material contrast focuses on rusted metal versus luminous stardust. Character design organizes conflict around a knight, sorceress, tyrant king, and experimental monster.

主要角色Main Characters

Sir Laine / 莱恩 — 王国骑士长，从服从命令到发现真相并最终反叛旧秩序。Knight Captain who evolves from obedience to rebellion after uncovering the truth.

Eileen / 艾琳 — 守护“反星之铁”的女巫，引导莱恩看清旧秩序的虚伪。Sorceress guarding the Iron Against the Stars, guiding Laine toward the truth.

King Darius / 大流士 — 企图以怪物与神圣权威建立恐怖统治的暴君。A tyrant king seeking rule through monsters and sacred authority.

No.319 Monster / 319 号怪物 — 教会实验产物，象征失控的神权与扭曲的秩序。An experimental church creature representing distorted order and failed divinity.
