Trespassing the White Tiger Hall

误入白虎堂

这是《水浒传》中林冲命运的转折点。改编时，我把"白虎堂"重构为一个纯粹的秩序场：纸艺甲胄、几何空间、冷白光线，让"清白"成为叙事与品牌的共同锚点。 This is the turning point of Lin Chong's fate in Water Margin. In the adaptation, the White Tiger Hall is restaged as a pure space of order: paper-crafted armor, geometric architecture, and cool-white light let "clarity" become the shared anchor between narrative and brand.

改编视角Adaptation Angle

"误入白虎堂"是林冲被诬陷的关键转折。改编时，我没有强调阴谋，而是把重心放到"被指控者的清白"上。空间几何化、光线冷白化，让画面本身具有审判感，而角色的每一次抬头、握剑、伸手都是"被证明清白"的路径。The White Tiger Hall chapter marks the moment Lin Chong is framed. Rather than emphasizing conspiracy, I refocus the adaptation on "the accused person's clarity." Space becomes geometric, light turns cool white, giving the image itself a courtroom quality. Every look up, sword grip, and outstretched hand becomes a step in the path of vindication.

这个视角刚好与云南白药"光钻白 · 光净"的产品语言对齐：清白不是被解释出来的，是被空间与光呈现出来的。This angle aligns naturally with Yunnan Baiyao's "Pro Shining White · Clean" product language: clarity is not explained — it is revealed by space and light.

我的职责与工作内容My Responsibilities

• 负责改编视角与叙事段落划分，把"被诬"转向"清白"的叙事骨架。I defined the adaptation angle and narrative beats, pivoting the story from "being framed" to "clarity."

• 负责白虎堂几何空间、纸艺甲胄与冷白光线的美术方向。I directed the art of the geometric hall, paper-crafted armor, and cool-white lighting.

• 负责关键帧生成、镜头设计与产品出现节奏。I generated keyframes, designed shots, and paced the entrance of the product.

• 负责提示词撰写与图生视频调试，控制甲胄质感、灯光冷度与镜头呼吸感。I wrote and tuned prompts for image-to-video, controlling armor texture, light temperature, and breathing motion.

• 负责剪辑、音乐节奏与品牌收束镜头。I handled editing, music pacing, and the brand-resolution shot.

主视觉Key Visual

短片主视觉：纸艺武将双手托起云南白药牙膏，构成"清白 × 守护"的仪式化画面。The film's key visual: paper-crafted armored hands presenting Yunnan Baiyao toothpaste — a ceremonial framing of "clarity × protection."

镜头与视觉系统Shot Language & Visual System

镜头语言Shot Language

以对称构图与轻微俯视角建立秩序感，情绪推进时切换到手部与武器的特写，让"清白"以物理动作被证明，而不是台词。Symmetrical framing and a slight overhead angle establish order; emotional beats cut to hand and weapon close-ups, letting "clarity" be proven through physical action, not dialogue.

材质与颜色Material & Palette

武将甲胄使用暗蓝纸艺 + 铜色铆钉，与云南白药"光钻白"淡蓝包装形成视觉呼应；主光源冷白略偏青，空间大面积压暗，让白色产品成为画面唯一的"清"点。The general's armor uses dark-blue paper craft with copper studs, echoing the pale blue of Pro Shining White packaging. Cool-white key light with a subtle cyan cast, and heavily shaded surroundings, let the white product become the only "clean" point in the frame.

制作流程Process

01段落提炼Chapter Distillation

选出林冲被引入白虎堂的四段关键节奏，作为改编骨架。Selecting the four key beats of Lin Chong being led into the hall as the adaptation spine.

02空间几何化Spatial Geometry

重新设计白虎堂为对称几何空间，让秩序感成为主叙事。Redesigning the hall as a symmetrical geometric space so order becomes the main narrative.

03纸艺造型Paper Craft Design

为角色打造纸艺甲胄，控制布料、铠甲与手部关节的可信度。Designing paper-craft armor — cloth, plating, and hand-joint credibility.

04分镜与关键帧Boards & Keyframes

先分镜再关键帧，让"审判感"贯穿全片。Boarding first, then keyframes, keeping the courtroom quality across the film.

05AI 动画AI Animation

提示词与图生视频，推进甲胄呼吸感与冷白光变化。Prompts and image-to-video drive armor breathing and cool-white light shifts.

06品牌收束Brand Resolution

在最后镜头把产品与"清白"合并，作为品牌落点。The final shot merges product with "clarity" as the brand landing.

《误入白虎堂》是"云南白药 × 水浒传"系列的第二支短片，主题为"清白与秩序"。"Trespassing the White Tiger Hall" is the second short in the Yunnan Baiyao × Water Margin series, themed around "clarity and order."
